mool a day (web · Toss · iOS · Android) · Effective November 20, 2026

3. Your choices and rights

  • Deleting the app or clearing the site’s data removes all game progress.
  • You can turn reminders off at any time in your device (or Toss) settings.
  • Ad tracking: on iOS use “Allow Apps to Request to Track”; on Android use “Delete advertising ID”. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK), you can change your choice any time under Ad privacy choices in the menu. Watching ads is always optional.
  • Because play logs and shared images are not linked to you, we generally cannot find one person’s records. To remove a shared image, email us the share link.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object; contact us and we will respond within the legal time limits. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as defined by California law.

4. Children

mool is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea, or the age of digital consent where you live).

5. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS, server access is limited to the operator, and records are deleted automatically when their retention period ends.
